PROPOSAL-NORM (phase 1, every docs/issue-<n>/proposals/*.md): state scope/change description, a named risk (a concrete failure mode, never "risks exist"), a rollback/back-out path, and cite at least one source (URL or repo path) for every adopted-methodology claim. Do not write "risk: TBD" or a rollback section that says "revert if needed" without naming the actual revert mechanism. proposal-fields-gate.sh refuses a Write/Edit/MultiEdit to a proposal document missing any of these four; the refusal names the specific missing section — fix that section and retry, do not restate prose that was already there under a different heading to satisfy the gate's keyword match.
